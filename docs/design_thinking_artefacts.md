## 1. Empathise

### Target users
Students and young adults (survey age range 18–30, median 19) who use music streaming
at least several times a week (86% of respondents). 69% name Spotify as their primary
platform; 14% YouTube Music, 10% Other and 7% Apple Music.

### Empathy map (composite, built from real open-text answers)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Says** | "Ads, remove ads" · "Poor recommendations" · "Repetitive recommendations" · "understand my music taste better" · "Organise all my music according to its vibe" · "change different suggestions that differ for mood and not on time" |
| **Thinks** | "Why is it showing me the same songs again?" · "I liked that track — will I ever find it again?" · "I want music for this mood, not what the algorithm thinks is popular." |
| **Does** | 86% add a song to a playlist or save/like it. 31% discover new music *outside* the app. 45% sometimes struggle to find songs saved a long time ago. |
| **Feels** | Annoyed by interruptions (52% mention ads/paywall). Mildly satisfied but not delighted by recommendations: 41% say the app understands their taste only "somewhat", 10% "poorly". |

### Key pain points, ranked by evidence
1. **Ads / paywall — 15/29 (52%)** — highest frequency, but **out of scope** for a client-side utility; recorded as a limitation.
2. **Recommendations / taste — 11/29 (38%)** — in scope: repetitive, not matching taste, not mood-sensitive.
3. **Saved-song retrieval — 13/29 (45%) sometimes struggle** — in scope: the data layer already exists.
4. **Vibe-finding friction — 14/29 (48%) rate Average or Difficult** — in scope but weaker evidence.
5. UI/customisation — 6/29 (21%) — future scope.

## 2. Define

### User persona (composite — not a real individual)

> **Aarav, 19 — B.Tech student, daily Spotify listener**
> - Listens several hours a day, mostly on mobile/web between classes.
> - Finds new music through friends and social media rather than inside the app (matches the 31% "outside the app" finding).
> - Saves tracks to playlists but rarely revisits them because search is tedious.
> - Wants recommendations that match his current mood and feels the app only "somewhat" understands his taste.
> - Frustrated by ads but knows a free user cannot remove them; wants more control instead.

### Point of View (POV)
> A daily music-streaming listener who saves tracks but struggles to rediscover them,
> and who feels recommendations only *somewhat* match their taste, needs control over
> how familiar or exploratory suggestions are and a way to revisit saved music by mood —
> because discovery currently happens outside the app and saved collections are hard to navigate.

### How Might We (HMW)
> **How might we** give listeners control over the familiarity of recommendations and
> let them rediscover saved music by mood, without requiring a paid subscription or a new account?

### MoSCoW prioritisation

| Must | Should | Could | Won't |
|---|---|---|---|
| Detect current track on Spotify / YouTube Music / Apple Music | Find by Vibe with platform-specific tags | Understand section (lyrics/meaning) | Remove ads / bypass paywall |
| Discover with Familiar / Balanced / Exploratory levels | My Preferences (discovery level, vibe, tracking toggle) | AI chat personalisation (one respondent request) | Download songs for offline use |
| Save recommendations locally and show them in My Music | Usage-event logging for Stage 5 testing | Cross-platform account sync | Native mobile app |
| Responsive dashboard + Python backend + SQLite | SUS and task timing | Separate native extension store listing | Paid subscription features |

### Statistical insight behind the problem
One-sample t-tests against neutral (2/3):

| Measure | Mean | t | p | 95% CI | Reading |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Vibe-finding ease | 2.38 | 2.81 | 0.0090 | [2.10, 2.66] | significantly above neutral |
| Taste understanding | 2.38 | 3.02 | 0.0054 | [2.12, 2.64] | above neutral, but 52% only "somewhat"/"poorly" |
| Saved retrieval | 2.03 | 0.18 | 0.8564 | [1.65, 2.42] | not significantly different |

**Honest framing:** the closed questions show a *good-but-not-great* baseline, not a
broken one. The opportunity is refinement of recommendations and retrieval, backed by
the open-text complaints — not a claim that users are deeply dissatisfied.

## 3. Ideate

### Mind map (text form)

```
Personal Music Experience
├── Discovery control
│   ├── Familiar / Balanced / Exploratory
│   ├── Based on current track
│   └── Save recommendations
├── Mood navigation
│   ├── Vibe tags
│   └── Platform-specific vibe sets
├── Personal library
│   ├── Saved songs list
│   ├── Remove / organise
│   └── Retrieval by mood
├── Understanding
│   ├── Lyrics
│   └── Artist backstory
└── Preferences
    ├── Default discovery level
    └── Usage tracking consent
```

### 10 generated ideas

| # | Idea | Desirability | Feasibility | Viability |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Familiar/Balanced/Exploratory discovery slider | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 2 | Vibe-tag recommendation browser | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 3 | Save button on every recommendation | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 4 | Personal library page listing saved tracks | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 5 | Current-track-aware recommendation context | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 6 | Preference panel for default level and vibe | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| 7 | Usage-event logging + SUS study page | 3 | 5 | 5 |
| 8 | Lyrics / song-meaning "Understand" panel | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| 9 | AI chatbot for personalised suggestions | 4 | 1 | 2 |
| 10 | In-app ad blocker | 5 | 1 | 1 |

### SWOT

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Works across three platforms without APIs or login | Client-side selectors are fragile if sites change |
| Fully local/private data | Demo recommendation database, not a real recommender |
| Clear, testable features | Apple Music had zero survey representation |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|
| Companion web dashboard adds backend + measurement | 29 responses is below the brief's target of 30 |
| Saved-song gap directly supported by data | Ads complaint is out of scope |
| SUS + timed tasks give quantitative validation | Single-contributor scope reduces teamwork marks |

### Selection matrix (weighted: desirability 40%, feasibility 35%, viability 25%)

| Idea | D | F | V | Weighted | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Discovery slider (1) | 5 | 5 | 5 | **5.00** | Build — top-ranked pain point |
| Vibe tags (2) | 5 | 5 | 5 | **5.00** | Build — 44% report friction |
| Save + My Music (3,4) | 5 | 5 | 5 | **5.00** | Build — 44% retrieval difficulty |
| Preferences (6) | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4.65 | Build |
| Usage logging + SUS (7) | 3 | 5 | 5 | 4.20 | Build — required by brief |
| Current-track context (5) | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4.00 | Keep (already built) |
| Understand (8) | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2.80 | Future scope |
| AI chatbot (9) | 4 | 1 | 2 | 2.45 | Future scope |
| Ad blocker (10) | 5 | 1 | 1 | 2.80 | Reject — out of scope |

**Final concept:** a privacy-first, cross-platform personalisation layer — Discover,
Find by Vibe, and My Music at its core, with a responsive companion dashboard,
usage measurement, and a stated limitation on ads.

### Written hypotheses (fixed before prototype testing)

- **H₁a (retrieval time):** Median time to find a saved song is lower with the
  dashboard's My Music page than with the participant's current method.
- **H₀a:** There is no difference in median retrieval time.
- **H₁b (taste satisfaction):** Mean rating of "the app understands my taste" is
  higher after using Familiar/Balanced/Exploratory discovery than the survey baseline
  of 2.38.
- **H₀b:** Mean rating is unchanged from 2.38.
- **H₁c (usability):** Mean SUS score of the prototype exceeds 68 (above-average benchmark).
- **H₀c:** Mean SUS score is 68 or lower.
- Significance level α = 0.05. Paired design (same users try both), Wilcoxon signed-rank
  for time if non-normal, otherwise paired t-test; one-sample t-test for SUS.

## 6. Appendix — interview guide (prepared, not administered)

1. Which app do you use most, and how often?
2. Walk me through the last time you looked for a new song. Where did you find it?
3. Tell me about a time you could not find a song you had saved.
4. How do you choose music for a specific mood?
5. What bothers you most about your current app?
6. If you could change one thing, what would it be and why?

this report comes from the survey's three open-ended questions. This is a known
